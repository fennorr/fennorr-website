Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Fennorr East Private Limited ("Fennorr", "we", "us" or "our") handles information in connection with 15, our productivity application.

1. About 15

15 is a productivity application designed to help users start tasks by focusing on short periods of focused activity.

2. Information stored by the app

15 may store information you enter or generate while using the app, such as task descriptions, session dates and times, session duration, completion choices and interactions with productivity features.

The core session history is designed to be stored locally on your device. Fennorr does not receive the contents of your task history simply because you use the app.

3. Subscriptions and payments

If you purchase a subscription or other in-app purchase, payment is processed by Apple through the App Store. Fennorr does not receive your full payment-card details.

We may use a subscription-management service to determine whether premium features are available to your installation and to manage subscription status. Such providers may process technical purchase and device-related identifiers necessary to provide this service.

4. Analytics and diagnostics

We may receive limited technical information necessary to operate, maintain and improve the app, such as crash or diagnostic information, where this is provided through the App Store or services used to operate the app.

5. How we use information

Information processed in connection with 15 is used to provide and maintain the app, enable purchased features, troubleshoot technical problems, improve reliability and comply with applicable legal obligations.

6. Selling personal information

Fennorr does not sell your personal information.

7. Third-party services

15 may rely on third-party services necessary for distribution, payments, subscription management, diagnostics or other app functionality. Those providers process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable law.

8. Data retention and deletion

Information stored locally by 15 remains on your device until it is removed through the app, the app's data is cleared, or the app is uninstalled, subject to the functionality available in the relevant version of the app.

If you have a privacy or deletion request concerning information held by Fennorr, you may contact us using the address below.

9. Children's privacy

15 is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app where parental consent would be required by applicable law.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app develops or applicable requirements change. The current version will be published on this page with its latest revision date.
